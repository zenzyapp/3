Daily wellbeing support for children

Calm Kids helps children practise feelings, calm routines and confidence every day.

Calm Kids is a browser-based wellbeing app designed for child-friendly emotional check-ins, calming activities, sleep support, social learning missions and gentle progress through Zenzy virtual pet care.

What children can do in Calm Kids

  • Complete a daily check-in and write about how they feel.
  • Use calming activities such as square breathing, grounding and meditation.
  • Practise gratitude, movement and positive self-talk.
  • Work through missions focused on emotions, communication and friendship.
  • Care for Zenzy, a virtual pet that responds to feeding, play and sleep routines.

Meet Zenzy

Built for calm, repeatable routines

The experience is structured around small, repeatable activities that can fit into daily routines at home, in classrooms or as part of supported emotional regulation practice.

  • Short activities reduce overwhelm.
  • Visual prompts help children stay engaged.
  • Rewards and pet care support habit formation.
  • Privacy stays simple because progress is stored locally in the browser.

Who Calm Kids is for

Calm Kids can support children who benefit from structured reflection, emotional vocabulary, regulation prompts, playful reinforcement and a predictable wellbeing routine.

It can also be useful for families, teachers and support staff looking for a child-friendly wellbeing tool that feels simple and encouraging.
