Emotional awareness for children

A simple daily feelings check-in can help children recognise emotions with less pressure.

The Calm Kids daily check-in guides children to choose how they feel, name a reason, and move into a short sequence of wellbeing activities. This gives emotional language a regular place in the day.

Try the app Back to overview

What the check-in includes

  • Feeling choices such as happy, okay, sad, angry and overwhelmed.
  • A prompt to explain why the feeling may be showing up.
  • Follow-up wellbeing steps like mindfulness, gratitude, movement and positive reflection.

Why daily emotional check-ins matter

Children often need repeated, low-pressure opportunities to notice emotions and connect them to real events. A short check-in routine can make those conversations feel normal instead of heavy.

  • It supports emotional vocabulary.
  • It builds a pause before reacting.
  • It creates space for reflection without demanding long writing.
  • It can support home or classroom routines.

Designed to feel manageable

Calm Kids pairs check-ins with playful progression. Instead of leaving a child with a feeling label alone, the app moves into structured next steps and gentle rewards.

How the daily check-in flows in Calm Kids

Step Purpose
Choose a feeling Helps a child pause and identify their current emotion.
Add a reason Connects the feeling to a situation, thought or event.
Complete guided activities Builds a practical response through breathing, movement, gratitude or reflection.
Earn progress Rewards consistency through stars, ZP and Zenzy progress.
