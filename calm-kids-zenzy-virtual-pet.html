Playful motivation through care

Zenzy gives Calm Kids a virtual pet that makes wellbeing routines feel more rewarding.

Zenzy is part companion, part progress system. Children can adopt, hatch, feed, play with and care for Zenzy while earning ZP through activities and missions in the app. This helps connect self-care actions with visible progress.

Open Zenzy Back to overview

What children can do with Zenzy

  • Collect and hatch a Zenzy egg.
  • Adopt Zenzy and choose a name.
  • Feed, hydrate, play with and help Zenzy sleep.
  • Build rooms, decorate habitats and unlock more routines.
  • Use mini-games and missions to support pet care progress.

Why a virtual pet can support engagement

For many children, routines feel easier to repeat when there is a visible companion and a clear reward loop. Zenzy adds that layer without replacing the core wellbeing work.

  • Progress feels concrete.
  • Care actions link naturally to emotional habits.
  • The app experience feels playful rather than purely instructional.

Linked to wellbeing, not separate from it

Zenzy is woven into the Calm Kids system. Daily check-ins, missions and regulation activities all feed into the pet care loop, which helps maintain momentum across the wider app experience.
