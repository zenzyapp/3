🧘 Meditate

Sit comfortably, close your eyes, and breathe slowly. 🌬️

🌿 💛 ☁️
  1. Start with a big, soft breath. Breathe in like you're smelling a yummy cookie, then breathe out like you're blowing out a candle.
  2. Notice your toes. Wiggle them a little and imagine they are warm and relaxed, like in a cozy blanket.
  3. Move to your legs. Let them feel heavy and calm, like they are sinking into a soft cloud.
  4. Now your tummy. Place a hand there if you want. Feel it rise and fall as you breathe slowly.
  5. Bring your attention to your hands. Give them a tiny squeeze, then let them go loose and floppy.
  6. Notice your shoulders. Let them drop down, as if you are taking off a heavy backpack.
  7. Finally, your face. Relax your jaw, your cheeks, and even your eyebrows. Take one more slow breath, then gently open your eyes.

How did you feel afterwards?

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

Introduction

Calm Kids is committed to protecting the privacy of children. This app is designed for children and complies with children's privacy protection standards.

Information We Collect

Calm Kids stores all data locally on your device only. We do NOT collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The app works entirely offline.

Data Storage

All progress, entries, and settings are saved locally in your browser's storage. This data remains on your device and is never sent to any server or third party.

What We Store Locally

  • Daily check-in progress
  • Activity completions and stars earned
  • Zen Points (ZP) balance
  • App settings and preferences
  • Pet customization choices

Data Deletion

You can delete all app data at any time using the "Reset App" button in Settings. This will clear all locally stored information.

Third-Party Services

This app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

This app is designed specifically for children and does not collect any personal information. No registration, account creation, or personal details are required to use the app.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact the app developer through your app store.
