Child-friendly regulation support

Calm Kids includes practical calm-down tools, grounding activities and bedtime support.

The app brings together short wellbeing tools for moments when children feel dysregulated, worried, upset, restless or ready for sleep. Each activity is framed in a simple, encouraging way that feels usable rather than clinical.

Open calm tools Back to overview

Included regulation activities

  • Deep breathing and square breathing.
  • 5-4-3-2-1 grounding.
  • Movement and exercise prompts.
  • Meditation, visualisation and calming body activities.
  • Sleep support through music, meditation and story options.

Grounding

Grounding prompts help children shift attention back to the present moment using senses and structured observation.

Movement

Movement-based tools give children an active option when sitting still is not the right fit for regulation.

Bedtime support

Calm Kids includes child-friendly routes into bedtime through quiet listening, meditation and story routines.

How these tools can help

  • They give children specific actions to take instead of a vague instruction to calm down.
  • They allow different regulation styles, including still, sensory and active strategies.
  • They support repeat practice, which matters more than a single activity.

Sleep routines in the app

Calm Kids also supports children who need a softer transition into rest. Sleep screens are connected to Zenzy care, which can make bedtime routines feel more engaging and predictable.
